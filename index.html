Core Modules

Everything an SME
needs to stay on top

BizTrack was built around the real daily pain points of Ugandan business owners — manual spreadsheets, scattered records, and zero visibility into what's actually happening in the business.

📊
Live Analytics Dashboard

Real-time revenue, expense, and profit charts. Filter by day, week, month, or custom date range. Trend lines with month-over-month comparisons automatically calculated.

🧾
Invoice & Billing

Create professional invoices in seconds, send via email or WhatsApp, and track payment status. Automated reminders for overdue invoices reduce late payments by 40%.

💳
Expense Tracking

Log expenses by category with receipt photo uploads. Recurring expenses auto-log monthly. Visual breakdown by staff, department, or project with budget alerts.

👥
Staff Management

Employee profiles, attendance tracking, payroll calculations, and performance records. Multi-role access — owner, manager, and staff see only what they need to.

📦
Inventory Management

Track stock levels, set low-stock alerts, and link inventory to sales automatically. Supports barcode scanning and generates purchase order suggestions when stock runs low.

📄
Automated Reporting

One-tap PDF and Excel reports — monthly P&L, tax summary, payroll sheet, and stock report. Share directly via email, WhatsApp, or save to Google Drive.
